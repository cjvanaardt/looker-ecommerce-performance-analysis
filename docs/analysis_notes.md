Q2 Notes
- No observed relationship between
	- Age and long term value
	- Number of orders and total profit, total revenue.
	- Traffic source has no effect on long term customer value.
- Observed relationship between
	- As number of orders increases, the aov tends to decrease.
	- Shorter time to second order tends to have a larger total revenue, aov, total profit
	- Clear linear relationship between total revenue and total profit. Only investigating one should be sufficient. Since brief mentions rising acquisition costs we are interested in profit, not just revenue.

- Men have a larger long term value (all measures) than Women. Same observation for most common department. This is expected since there is a one to one relationship between gender and most common department.
- Austria and Colombia users have lower long term value compared to other countries while users from Germany have higher long term value (although this is just from one observation).
- The total profit of users with more orders is larger on average than those with fewer. Despite this the average order value and average order cost doesn't change. Hence individual orders from repeat customers aren't more profitable than orders from new ones.
- The total profit and revenue for users with smaller time to second order is slightly larger on average than those with larger time to second order. This seems related to the insight about the number of orders. Indeed. Users with a smaller time to second order have more orders on average than those with a larger time to second order.
- Users with a larger first order revenue tend to have a larger long term value. This is expected. The important question is this just because the first order has a larger revenue, or do customers with large first order revenues tend have different purchasing behaviour than customers is general.
- Customers with most common categories of expensive clothing items like outerwear & coats, suits & sport coats, etc have higher long term value than those of cheaper clothing items like socks and underwear.
